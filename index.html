Let's talk
How far ahead should we book?(01)

Campaign shoots usually need three to six weeks so we can recce, plan and clear crew and aircraft. Smaller stills days can often happen within a fortnight. If your date is fixed, get in touch early and we'll hold it.

Where do you shoot?(02)

We're based in the Lake District and work across the UK and abroad. Travel and accommodation are quoted up front, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Are your drone and FPV pilots licensed and insured?(03)

Yes. Our pilots fly with current CAA authorisation and we carry public liability cover. For FPV and complex locations we plan the flight and any permissions before the day. Ask and we'll send current certificates.

What do we get at the end?(04)

Graded stills and finished film cuts in the formats you need: campaign, web and social crops. Edit and colour are done in-house, so one team sees the job through.

How long until delivery?(05)

Stills are typically with you within two weeks of the shoot. Films depend on the edit, usually three to four weeks. Tight deadlines are possible if we know early.

Can you work with our agency or in-house team?(06)

Happily. We slot in alongside creative teams, directors and existing briefs, or run the whole production ourselves. Either way you get one point of contact.
